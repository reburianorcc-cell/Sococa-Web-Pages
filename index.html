What is SOCOCA?

SOCOCA is a mapping information cloud service that allows real-time data sharing through a smartphone application.

It seamlessly fits all industries, providing efficient mapping solutions tailored to your needs.

Benefits of Implementation

  • Sales activities can be streamlined, leading to increased sales through improved actions.
  • After returning to the office, there is no need to enter information or prepare documents, dramatically improving efficiency.
  • Customer information can be archived, ensuring a smooth transition and enhanced service.
  • Even individuals with limited IT skills can easily register text and photos, significantly reducing task time.
  • Registration items can be customized according to products and services at no additional cost.
